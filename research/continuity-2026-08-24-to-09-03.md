# LIU BEIHE Research Continuity — 24 Aug to 3 Sep 2026

This public research note reconstructs a ten-day period in which several strands of LIU BEIHE's practice became more clearly differentiated across publication, residency, exhibition and moving-image contexts.

## 1. Code, Computation, and Creative Agency

A manuscript route developed around the question of creative agency after generative systems. Rather than treating authorship as the production of an image from nothing, the research asks how artistic agency is redistributed across selection, refusal, sequencing, constraint and responsibility.

Related framework: **Authorship After Generation**.

## 2. The Cut Before the Cut

Generative systems do not only produce images; they also produce fields of candidates before editing begins. This changes montage. The editor no longer begins with a finite set of photographed shots but with a potentially expandable field of generated possibilities.

The research therefore asks whether editing now begins earlier: in prompting, rejection, iteration, model choice and the decision not to generate further.

Related frameworks: **Post-Camera Cinema**, **Authorship**, **Negative Craft**.

## 3. The Image After Evidence

A second strand focused on provenance and photographic trust. If photographic appearance no longer guarantees that a camera faced a prior event, then an image increasingly arrives with a history that must be reconstructed through metadata, process, context and institutional framing.

This leads to a shift from image-as-proof toward image-as-claim.

Related frameworks: **Synthetic Witnessing**, **Visual Evidence**, **Post-Camera Cinema**.

## 4. Visual Evidence as Public Installation

The research also moved beyond essays into installation logic. A public-facing digital work can treat evidence as a changing state rather than a stable property. Viewer behaviour, machine classification and temporal accumulation can alter what the work appears to certify.

This is the basis for a site-specific direction linking **Visual Evidence** with **Machine Seeing**.

## 5. Beyond Human / Interdependence

For museum and residency contexts, machine perception is treated not as a neutral tool but as another situated mode of seeing. The research asks how human and nonhuman systems assign salience, compress cultural difference and produce synthetic subject positions.

Related frameworks: **Machine Seeing**, **Nonhuman Salience**, **Cultural Compression**, **Synthetic Subjectivity**.

## 6. Public Identity as Research Infrastructure

During this period LIU BEIHE's public identity system was also formalized. The public role is defined as moving image artist / filmmaker working across generative image, experimental cinema and visual research.

The central proposition is:

> The image is not a record. It is an active producer of belief.

Generative technology is therefore used not as a stylistic filter but as a method for producing and testing visual credibility. Construction, damage, obstruction, withholding and repetition remain visible so that images can approach evidence while exposing their own construction.

## 7. Archive rule

This public archive contains research propositions, works, frameworks and public-facing documentation only. Private correspondence, contact information, unpublished application strategy and internal relationship records remain outside the repository.
