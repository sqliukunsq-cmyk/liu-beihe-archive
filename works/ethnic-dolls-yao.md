# Ethnic Dolls: YAO

## Research frame: Cultural Compression

*Ethnic Dolls: YAO* is a representative practice case in LIU BEIHE's research into cultural representation and machine legibility.

The work is connected to the framework **Cultural Compression**: the proposition that generative systems may condense culturally situated forms into recurring, statistically recognizable visual signals. The research asks what becomes highly legible to a model, what becomes generic, and what situated knowledge disappears in that translation.

### Research relations

### Methodological boundary

Generated resemblance is not evidence about Yao culture. No claim about costume, ritual, history, geography, identity, or tradition should be inferred from model output. Cultural and historical claims require independent sources.

The work is therefore positioned as practice-based research into representation and machine legibility, not as an ethnographic authority.

Production metadata and specific cultural-source claims are intentionally omitted until canonical documentation is verified.
