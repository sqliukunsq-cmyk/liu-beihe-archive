# Alien Friend

## Research frame: Nonhuman Gaze

*Alien Friend* is one of the representative works used in LIU BEIHE's current artistic and institutional positioning.

Within the research archive, the work is treated as a practice case for **Nonhuman Gaze**: an investigation of perception, misunderstanding, and the difficulty of imagining a viewpoint that cannot simply be reduced to ordinary human seeing.

### Research relations

- Nonhuman Gaze
- Nonhuman Salience
- Machine Seeing
- Interdependence

### Evidence boundary

The work is an artistic investigation of nonhuman perception and misrecognition. It is not presented as scientific evidence about extraterrestrial, animal, or machine perception.

Production metadata such as exact year, medium, duration, credits, workflow, and screening history is intentionally omitted until canonical documentation is verified.
