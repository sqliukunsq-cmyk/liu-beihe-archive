## Public research scope

- Post-Camera Cinema

- Cultural Compression

The central research question is not whether generative media can imitate photographic or cinematic images. It is how images can produce belief, testimony, memory, evidence and authorship when no photographed event necessarily precedes them.

## Research position

LIU BEIHE treats generative technology not as a visual style layer but as a method for producing and testing visual credibility. The practice examines how viewers complete incomplete testimony, how synthetic images acquire documentary force, and how directing changes when the image is selected from generated possibilities rather than recorded from a pre-existing scene.

This creates a research field connecting moving image, machine perception, synthetic memory, visual evidence and post-camera authorship.
