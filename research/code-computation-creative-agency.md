# Code, Computation, and Creative Agency

**LIU BEIHE / 刘北河 — research note, 2026**

## Abstract

When generative systems produce, rank, and pre-structure visual possibilities, creative agency is redistributed rather than erased. The central question is no longer whether a machine can originate an image, but where judgment enters a computational workflow: generation, selection, refusal, sequencing, provenance, and responsibility.

This research frames authorship after generation as a structure of accountable judgment. Candidate abundance shifts artistic labor away from simple image production toward decisions about what should remain unseen, what should enter a sequence, and what consequences follow from the selected image.

## Working propositions

1. Generation is not equivalent to authorship.
2. Selection is a creative operation when the candidate field is abundant.
3. Refusal can be a compositional act rather than a failed generation.
4. Sequencing restores temporal and relational authorship.
5. Provenance and responsibility become part of the work when synthetic images inherit photographic credibility.
