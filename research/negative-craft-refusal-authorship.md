# Negative Craft: Refusal, Selection, and Authorship

**LIU BEIHE / 刘北河 — research framework, 2026**

When generative systems make image production abundant, craft moves partly from making to refusing. **Negative Craft** names the artistic labor of deleting, withholding, rejecting, and narrowing generated possibilities.

## Four operations

1. **Selection** — choosing among candidates.
2. **Refusal** — rejecting technically successful but conceptually false results.
3. **Sequencing** — producing meaning through temporal arrangement.
4. **Responsibility** — owning the consequences of the selected image.

## Authorship after generation

Abundance does not remove authorship. It changes where authorship becomes visible. Authorship can be understood less as sole origination and more as accountable judgment over a field of possibilities.

This distinction matters because the language of “curation” can be too weak for generative practice. Refusal is not simply taste applied after production. It can determine the conceptual boundary of the work before an image becomes public.

## Method boundary

This framework does not override the authorship or AI-use requirements of any publication, institution, or collaborator. Where a venue requires a non-AI writing process, that condition must be respected independently of this research framework.
