# Cultural Compression: MAH Research Method

## Research question

What happens when culturally situated visual forms become legible to generative systems primarily through recurring statistical signals, and how can artistic practice make visible what this compression leaves out?

## Working architecture

1. **Cultural Compression** — situated difference becomes reusable visual probability.
2. **Machine Legibility** — recognizability to a model is separated from cultural knowledge.
3. **Practice case: Ethnic Dolls: YAO** — used only within documented evidence boundaries.
4. **Controlled comparison** — recurring motifs, counterexamples, verified reference/archive material, and generation histories.
5. **Map of Absence** — record what disappears, becomes generic, or is replaced by familiar shorthand.
6. **Selection Reflexivity** — examine how the artist’s own choices may reproduce the compression being criticized.
7. **Conclusion** — machine legibility is not cultural understanding.

## Evidence protocol

Model outputs, artist interpretation, historical/cultural sources, and external scholarship must remain distinguishable. Generated imagery is never treated as evidence about Yao culture. Cultural Compression, Machine Legibility, and Map of Absence are maintained here as LIU BEIHE working frameworks unless independently published elsewhere.

A final scholarly bibliography will include only verified editions and sources actually used in the paper.
