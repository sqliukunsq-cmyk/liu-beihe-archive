# Does AI Know It Is AI?

## Research frame: Synthetic Subjectivity

This work is part of LIU BEIHE's current moving-image research into generated identity, memory effects, perception, and the instability of visual evidence.

Its central question — “Does AI know it is AI?” — is used as an artistic problem, not as a scientific claim that a generative system is conscious. The work studies how generated images can produce the *effect* of a viewpoint or interiority even when no experiencing witness stands behind the image.

### Evidence boundary

Statements that an AI “knows,” “remembers,” or “experiences” should be understood only as questions of image construction, fiction, or spectator perception unless independently supported by scientific evidence.
