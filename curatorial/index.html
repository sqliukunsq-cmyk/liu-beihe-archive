Curatorial
Entry Points

Four routes through LIU BEIHE’s moving-image practice, connecting works with the research questions that produce them.

This page is a curatorial index rather than an exhibition-history claim. It is designed to help institutions quickly understand how works, methods and research frameworks relate.

1. Post-Camera Cinema & Synthetic Witnessing

What changes when a moving image keeps the sensory grammar of photography while no photographed event necessarily precedes it? This route focuses on the changing relation between image, capture, provenance and testimony.

Work

Does AI Know It Is AI?

Synthetic subjectivity and the cinematic production of apparent interiority.

Research

The Image That Never Happened

Capture event, generation event, narrative event, provenance and evidentiary sensation.

2. Machine Seeing & Nonhuman Salience

This route asks how computational systems organise attention before the artist makes a final decision: which bodies, gestures, relations and visual signs become probable, recognisable or central.

Work

Alien Friend

Nonhuman perception, misunderstanding and the gap between record accuracy and interpretive adequacy.

Research

Nonhuman Salience as a Directing Method

Directing as the scrutiny and redistribution of machine-ranked attention.

3. Cultural Compression & Machine Legibility

How does a complex cultural reality become narrowed into a smaller vocabulary of high-probability visual signs? This route looks at recognisability, archives, representation and what generated images repeatedly leave out.

Work

Ethnic Dolls: YAO

A project concerned with cultural probability, legibility and the risks of visually compressing identity.

Research

Cultural Compression in Generative Images

A practice-based study of repeated signs, missing ordinary life and the difference between recognisability and evidence.

4. Negative Craft & Authorship

When plausible images become abundant, authorship can move toward judgment: selection, deletion, delay, arrangement, provenance and refusal. This route treats the rejected candidate as part of the artistic process rather than invisible waste.

Method

Negative Craft

Authorship through deletion, limitation, non-selection and accountable refusal under conditions of generative abundance.

Research

After the Prompt

Judgment, provenance, pre-curation and the artist’s right to refuse.
