## Short bio

LIU BEIHE is a moving image artist and filmmaker working across generative image, experimental cinema and visual research.

## Practice statement

The practice examines how images produce belief when witnesses, places, and archives are absent. Construction, damage, withholding, and repetition form the core working method.

**THE IMAGE IS NOT A RECORD. IT IS AN ACTIVE PRODUCER OF BELIEF.**

The work builds visual conditions of events that might have happened rather than presenting synthetic images as evidence of events that did happen. Generative technology is used as a method for producing and testing visual credibility.

## Working method

**CONSTRUCT / DAMAGE / WITHHOLD / REPEAT**
