# Research Continuity — 18–23 August 2026

This public note reconstructs a documented period in LIU BEIHE / 刘北河's research development. It is a retrospective archive entry created on 3 September 2026. Private correspondence, contact details, unpublished manuscript text, and internal application status are intentionally excluded.

## Research lines active during this period

### Cultural Compression
A practice-based research line on how generative systems render culturally complex material machine-legible by compressing visual difference into recurring tokens, surfaces, motifs, and categories. During this period it was developed toward an academic-conference format rather than treated only as an artist statement.

### Synthetic Witnessing / Visual Evidence
A research line asking what happens when images appear evidentiary without originating in a photographed event. The emphasis shifted toward provenance, verification, visual credibility, and the relation between artistic practice and scholarly research.

### Post-Camera Cinema
A framework for cinema in which the image is no longer necessarily downstream from a photographed scene. The directing question becomes less about recording what happened and more about selecting, sequencing, refusing, and structuring candidate images.

### Negative Craft / Authorship
Under conditions of generative abundance, authorship is located not only in making but in judgment: refusal, deletion, selection, sequencing, constraint, and responsibility.

### Nonhuman Salience
A directing and image-theory problem concerned with what computational systems make perceptually or culturally salient, and how this differs from human-centered attention.

### Synthetic Memory / Digital Archaeology
Generative moving image was explored as an archive condition rather than a simple simulation of the past: a way to study how future images may reorganize memory, historical imagination, and authority.

## Works and research objects

- **QIN SHI HUANG** continued to function as a moving-image research object for synthetic memory, historical imagination, and generated reality.
- **Ethnic Dolls: YAO** remained connected to questions of cultural provenance, representation, and compression.
- **Does AI Know It Is AI?** informed the developing inquiry into machine subjectivity and image problems.
