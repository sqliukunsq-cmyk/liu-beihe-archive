Positioning

LIU BEIHE is an independent moving-image artist and filmmaker working across cinema, photography, generative moving image and artistic research. His practice examines what happens to directing, authorship and visual evidence when images no longer need to begin with a photographed event. Rather than treating generative AI as a production shortcut, he works with it as a condition that changes how images are selected, trusted, refused and attributed.

LIU BEIHE / 刘北河 is an independent moving-image artist and filmmaker working across cinema, photography, generative moving image and artistic research. Extended Bio

LIU BEIHE / 刘北河 is an independent moving-image artist and filmmaker whose work moves between cinema, photography, generative moving image and artistic research. His current practice asks how directing changes when the image is no longer anchored to a photographed event, and how authorship, provenance, testimony and perception are reorganised by generative systems. Across works including Alien Friend, Does AI Know It Is AI? and Ethnic Dolls: YAO, he develops recurring lines of inquiry around nonhuman perception, synthetic subjectivity, cultural legibility and the changing evidentiary status of images. His research vocabulary includes Post-Camera Cinema, Machine Seeing, Synthetic Witnessing, Visual Evidence, Nonhuman Salience and Cultural Compression.
